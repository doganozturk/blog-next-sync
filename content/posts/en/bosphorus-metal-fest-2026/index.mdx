Last weekend, I went to Bosphorus Open Air Metal Fest at UNIQ İstanbul. Over two days, I saw familiar bands, discovered a few new ones, and took plenty of photos.

On the first day, I watched Fleshless, Kampfar, and I Am Morbid. I especially liked Fleshless. Their performance was one of the highlights of the weekend for me.

The open air setting worked really well at this time of year. The weather was good, and the logistics inside the festival area were great. There were food stands, plenty of bars with different drinks, good WCs, and places to sit when I needed a break between shows.

I think Bosphorus Open Air Metal Fest is one of the greatest festival organizations I have attended in Turkey. It is on my must-attend list now.
